Privacy Policy & Data Management for City Guest

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Markaz Knowledge City ("we", "our", or "us") operates the City Guest mobile application. This page outlines our data handling practices and procedures for requesting account deletion.

1. Information We Collect

City Guest is an internal guest relation and administrative task management system. We collect minimal administrative data required for operations, including:

  • Guest Information (Name, Phone Number, Occupation, Purpose of Visit, District, State)
  • Admin Account Details (Email Address, Name, Assigned Role)

2. How Information Is Used

The collected data is exclusively used for guest record keeping, task assignment follow-ups, and internal event management at Markaz Knowledge City.

3. Data Security & Protection

Data is stored securely on encrypted cloud infrastructure powered by Supabase. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third-party advertisers or unauthorized external entities.

4. Account & Data Deletion Requests

Users and administrators can request full account deletion and personal data removal at any time.

To request account deletion:

  1. Send an email to mdnaseef2004@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - City Guest".
  2. Include your registered email address and name.
  3. Your account and associated profile information will be permanently deleted from our servers within 48 hours. Guest visit logs created prior to deletion will have author attributes unlinked while preserving general visit records.
